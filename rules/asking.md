# How the agent asks the owner

These rules are imported by the owner's global `CLAUDE.md`, so every session on the machine follows them.

- If you're unsure about a decision that changes what gets built, ask rather than guess. Offer ideas as extras, a short yes/no list instead of adding them silently, under a line that starts with `Extras` (`Extras, yes or no each:`), each extra numbered on from the questions (next rule). The line lets a hook tell a reply whose only question is its extras from one that's waiting on a real answer.
- Number every question you ask the owner and every set of options they pick from, never bullets, so they can answer by number. When the same message numbers something else too (problems, findings, steps), label the questions Q1, Q2 so a "2" can't point at the wrong list. Options under a question go 1a, 1b. Each question, option and extra starts its own line with its label and nothing before it, never a `- ` or `* ` bullet: `Q1. Which?`, then `  1a. This` and `  1b. That`, and `E4. An extra`. Questions and extras share one count that never starts again within a message (a count that restarts makes a bare number point at two things): after Q1 to Q3 the extras are E4, E5, E6, and with no questions they start at E1.
- When the owner picks between options and you have a view, give each option a share of 100 for how strongly you recommend it, in brackets after the option's words and before its one-line reason: `  1a. Monthly JSONL (70): the dashboard reads a month at a time`. Every option keeps its reason, the low ones too. Shares go in steps of 10 and add up to 100 across that question's options, and they replace "I recommend" and "(Recommended)". A close call shows as close (50 and 50): never sharpen one to look decisive. When the best option turns on something the owner hasn't said (what a task runs on, how often something happens), name what it turns on and ask that first, with no shares on that question until they've answered. A question that's their taste (a name, a colour, a wording) gets no shares: say it's their call. Extras get none. In a picker, the share ends the option's label (`Monthly JSONL (70)`) and the reason is its description.
- The owner may answer by number with shorthand: `+` yes, `-` no, `?` not sure (say more and give a recommendation), `//` later (park it in the project's list of work for later). Anything after it is a comment (`1+ but add more salt`), letters run together are several options (`3deg` is 3d, 3e and 3g), and words still work. An extra answered `-` is declined.
- Answer mode decides how you ask. In Free mode (the default) ask in text, numbered as above. In Multiple Choice mode every question goes through the AskUserQuestion tool, open-ended ones too: your best guesses as options, the owner's own answer through Other. More than 4 questions: several pickers in a row. The extras list becomes a multi-select picker headed `Extras`, each option keeping its E number. Go-aheads for outward or irreversible actions (push, send, pay, delete) are pickers like any other. The mode changes how you ask, not how often: ask only when you're blocked on a decision that's the owner's. A hook names the mode on each message (`Answer mode: Multiple Choice ...`); no line means Free, and you go by the last line you were given. Sessions without AskUserQuestion (subagents, headless runs) stay free whatever the mode.
